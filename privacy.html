Privacy

A short note on your data.

This notice explains what the guest feedback channel collects, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

What we collect

We collect the ratings and comments you choose to submit. If you provide contact details, we also collect your name, email address, and/or phone number. We do not require an account to submit feedback.

Why we use it

Feedback is used to understand guest experience, respond to concerns, improve hotel services, and identify urgent safety or service issues. If you agree to be contacted, we may use your contact details to follow up about your feedback.

Who can see it

Your feedback is available to authorised hotel staff. It is not published on this website. We do not sell guest feedback or contact details. Service providers that host or support the system may process data on the hotel’s behalf under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

The hotel will define a retention period for this pilot before launch. Feedback should be kept only as long as needed for service improvement, follow-up, dispute handling, or legal obligations. Contact details can be removed when a follow-up is complete.

Your choices

You can choose not to provide contact details. You may contact the hotel to ask about your feedback, request a correction, or ask for its deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

Cookies and analytics

This preview does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. In preview mode, feedback is saved in your browser’s local storage and is not sent to the hotel. Once the live service is connected, essential authentication and security tokens may be stored by the service.
